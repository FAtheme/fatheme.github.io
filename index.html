About

I came to AI from engineering. The questions I care about most are about the people who use it.

Two roles shaped how I think about this. As a Head Teaching Assistant, I gave students one-to-one support, and I learned that the point where a student gets stuck often differs from the point the course design expects. During my product management internship, I saw that a service depends on psychology as much as on engineering. I loved that work. Later I completed a Behavioral Economics course (MCI Academy, April 2026) to understand that side better.

Research

My research centres on explaining the predictions of convolutional neural networks.

My MSc thesis at Kharazmi University (advisor: Prof. Mohammad Soltanian) introduced CGFI and was graded 19.75/20. It was published in Machine Learning and Knowledge Extraction with Prof. Keivan Borna and Prof. Luca Longo. A second manuscript with Prof. Longo applies CGFI to explaining fine-grained visual classifiers.
